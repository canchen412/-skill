② 强制前缀（每次必带，四段）

P1 · 画风与画幅

2D Japanese anime thick-paint rendering, soft airbrushed gradients building the form,
light-to-shadow transitions are SOFT edges, NOT hard lines.
16:9 (1536x864), full-bleed artwork, no letterbox bars.

P2 · IP 固定块（逐字复用，只换 POSE）

CHARACTER: a lean athletic anime boy, late-teen, short spiky black hair, warm tan skin,
defined jawline and expressive dark anime eyes WITH natural white sclera and catchlights,
slim athletic build NOT bulky, NOT Q-version, NOT skinny.
WARDROBE (must stay identical every time): a plain white ribbed tank top with a
round scoop neck, black drawstring jogger pants with white side stripes, bare feet.
Body build, face and hairstyle EXACTLY the same as reference image 1 — do NOT change
the outfit color, do NOT add sleeves, do NOT add shoes.
RENDERING LOCK: soft airbrushed 2D anime thick-paint EVEN UNDER DRAMATIC LIGHTING;
NO hard specular bars, NO hatching, NO thick black outlines, NO flat cel-shaded color
blocks, NO American superhero comic style, NO plastic CG/3D-render look, NO waxy skin.

P3 · 构图与视频就绪

COMPOSITION: subject at a golden-ratio intersection (never dead center), strong
diagonal energy flow, three depth planes (blurred foreground / sharp subject /
atmospheric background), generous clean negative space at the bottom quarter for captions.
FULL-BODY RULE (when the whole body is in frame): the figure is OFF-CENTER, occupies
the upper two-thirds, and his feet END WELL ABOVE the bottom edge — the bottom quarter
stays empty dark floor. Never let the feet touch the bottom frame edge.
MOTION-READY: at least one element that can move.
MIRROR-SAFE: any reflective surface must show a CONSISTENT, non-mirrored reflection.
DARK FLOOR LOCK (whenever a floor or mirror is in frame): DARK MATTE floor, no white
reflective floor, no glowing white ground plane — the bottom quarter must stay clean
and readable for captions.

N · 负向词

NEGATIVE: NO text, NO watermark, NO logo, NO brand packaging on the outfit,
NO extra limbs, NO six fingers, NO distorted hands, NO cluttered props,
NO busy background, NO flat empty backdrop, NO chibi, NO photorealistic 3D.

⑤ 生成后自检 + i2v 优先级

每条必查 5 项

检查项判定标准
服装白背心 + 黑束脚裤 + 赤脚，三件齐全、颜色没变、没加鞋
画风2D 厚涂；无白色硬高光条、无粗黑描边、无 3D 塑料感
脸五官正常、有动漫眼高光；没被产品 / 手臂挡住
反射镜中倒影姿态与本体一致，无镜像错位、无反写文字
底部留白底部 1/4 必须是深色干净区；若出现白亮地板/白色反光带，追加 DARK MATTE FLOOR 重 roll（实测 SK-06 首次就踩了）
视频就绪有可动之物（碎屑/热气/粉末/水花/光束/浊气/汗水）；底部 1/4 干净

#实测坑现象修法（已写进 P3）
1镜前地板变白亮块第一版底部出现一条白色高反射亮带，把字幕区吞掉P3 加 DARK FLOOR LOCK：DARK MATTE floor, no white reflective floor, no glowing white ground plane + 单独一行 FLOOR 段落，实测第二次就压住了
2主体变死居中、脚踩底边追加约束后模型把人物摆正到正中、双脚顶到下沿，底部留白被吃掉把 golden-ratio intersection 升级为 FULL-BODY RULE —— 人物必须"悬"在画面上，脚不落地
3碎裂镜倒影过多模型把碎片全填满他的脸（7–9 张），画面变"万脸墙"、冲击力被稀释限定 inside no more than THREE separate mirror shards the reflection is visible —— 碎片留空，纯黑反光面更有质感
用法提醒：这三条都属"追加式约束"，只在踩坑后加，一次全加容易让模型过度约束、动作僵硬。建议流程：先跑基础版 → 目视 → 哪条不对加哪条。
